Ecological Statistician

Kia ora — welcome to my personal page! I am an ecological statistician and researcher at Waipapa Taumata Rau | The University of Auckland, specialising in developing robust statistical frameworks for population estimation in POPAN capture-recapture models. Under the supervision of Professor Rachel Fewster, my current research focuses on population estimation of blue whales.

In early 2027, I will commence my PhD, expanding my research into the genetics of invasive predators. This will focus on developing statistical tools and tests to directly support biosecurity and conservation management efforts across Aotearoa New Zealand.

BSc(Hons) Research Supervised by Prof. Rachel Fewster

Addressing Individual Misidentification in POPAN Capture-Recapture Models

Photo-identification data is frequently vulnerable to individual misidentification, which creates artificial "ghost" signatures that systematically bias demographic trends. Using multi-decade eastern Australian humpback whale datasets sourced from Happywhale.com, this research developed a novel stochastic reallocation algorithm designed to repair corrupted capture histories.
